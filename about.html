03 : About me

Educational Background

Hamilton holds a strong multidisciplinary background in science and technology, anchored by dual Bachelor of Science degrees. He earned a B.S. in Software Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington in December 2021, building upon a previous B.S. in Chemistry completed in May 2013. During his chemistry studies, Hamilton contributed to a published academic journal, researching the optimization of trypsin proteolysis for mass spectrometry applications. His academic foundation began at Tarrant County College District, where he earned both an Associate of Arts and an Associate of Science in 2010. He graduated with a 3.80 GPA, earned Dean’s List recognition for two consecutive terms, and was inducted into the Phi Theta Kappa Honor Society. Today, Hamilton leverages his analytical and technical expertise across the technology, and telecommunications, specializing as a Senior Software Developer, Quality Assurance Analyst, and Data Validation Engineer.

Senior Software Engineer

Hamilton is a strategic and results-driven software engineering professional recognized for his leadership capabilities and collaborative approach. He excels at building strong team dynamics and partnering with cross-functional experts to deliver high-impact technical solutions. With comprehensive expertise across full-stack web development, cross-platform mobile applications, and rigorous data validation methodologies, Hamilton effectively bridges the gap between complex engineering and business execution. His technical foundation includes advanced proficiency in software quality assurance, back-end application development, production parallel/unit testing, and production data validation. By pairing technical excellence with strategic foresight, Hamilton consistently engineers innovative, secure, and scalable systems. Committed to continuous learning, he stays at the forefront of industry trends, seamlessly integrating cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and IoT advancements into complex technical environments.

Analytical Chemist

Between 2011 and 2017, Hamilton built a diverse foundation in the pharmaceutical and chemical sectors, demonstrating deep expertise across production manufacturing and quality control. His career began in compound manufacturing, specializing in the synthesis and production of pharmaceutical products. Transitioning into quality control and R&D, Hamilton implemented rigorous testing protocols, ensured strict compliance with GMP standards, and led development initiatives for both branded and custom-compounded medications. He also spent time in academia, where he conducted applied research and shared industry insights. Ultimately, his comprehensive background spans laboratory operations, regulatory compliance, and drug development.

Senior Developer-Data Validations

Since 2023, Hamilton work as a data validation enginer for Verizon Billing Operations. He is responsible for designing validation test methods, design automatation process validations for consumer and Business to Business records, authors detailed technical reports, and work with development team on Verizon internal applications and tools. Furthermore, his daily work focuses on ensuring the absolute accuracy, completeness, and reliability of critical data within Verizon’s enterprise billing infrastructure.
